🚲 City Bike Rental Demand Analysis

This project analyzes bike rental data in an urban setting to uncover what drives demand and how to enhance user satisfaction and system efficiency. Using hourly data that includes weather and calendar features, we identify actionable patterns and strategic opportunities for the bike-sharing business.

💡 Key Insights

1. Time & Context Matter: Peak rentals occur on weekends and holidays during daylight hours—primarily for leisure.
2. Weather Drives Demand: Rentals increase at comfortable temperatures (15–25°C), but drop drastically in poor weather.
3. Predictive Strategies Work: Weather and time data can power forecasts and marketing strategies.

✅ Recommendations

  • Optimize Fleet: Allocate bikes to high-demand zones in advance using predictive data.
  • Forecast Accurately: Incorporate weather and holiday patterns into planning tools.
  • Boost Off-Peak Usage: Use discounts and targeted promotions during low-traffic periods.
